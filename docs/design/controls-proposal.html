Proposed destinations. Values reflect observed Bose settings where known; these are not working controls in this sketch.

A quiet Home.

Keep the familiar layout. Status, three modes, four devices and the EQ summary. No new sections for occasional settings.

Tap to switch. Hold to edit. Go straight from a Home mode to its editor. No intermediate mode list or Manage button.

Preserve immediate mode switching, stable connection states and queued actions during refresh.

The Bose-style noise control.

A broad, rounded bar with a shaded level, fine ticks at its edges and the label inside. Drag anywhere along the bar to change the strength.

Quiet and Aware stay fixed. Their bar shows a lock. Focus exposes the adjustable bar and Wind Block.

Home still opens this editor with a long press. Save applies draft changes; Cancel returns without applying them.

The curve is the control.

Drag the three points vertically. The Bézier curve and numbers respond together, with no duplicate band sliders or adjustment buttons.

Large invisible touch targets make the small points easy to grab. In the native app, VoiceOver exposes adjustable values. Commit on release and show only confirmed settings after a failed write.

Useful, without crowding Home.

Native grouped rows, concise values and detail pages for longer choices.
